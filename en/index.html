For Training Companies

Four reasons to hire with us instead of searching alone

Replacement guarantee

If an apprentice drops out for reasons beyond the company's control, we provide a low-cost replacement within a defined period.

Active dropout-risk management

Regular check-ins in months 1, 3, 6 and 12 — problems are caught early, not once it's too late.

A focus on technical & industrial trades too

Mechatronics, electrical engineering, IT and more — a segment many placement agencies barely serve.

Transparency dashboard

Full visibility into applicant status, visa progress and training start — no black-box feeling.

For Applicants

What you can expect from us

Radical cost transparency

The placement is funded through the company's success fee — no hidden costs for you, ever.

Buddy & alumni programme

A personal point of contact and connection to apprentices already placed, before and after arrival.

Support beyond arrival

Regular check-ins throughout your entire training, not just support until you land.

Process

How the partnership works

Free initial consultation

Requirements profile: trade, pay scale, start date, number of positions.

Pre-screened candidate shortlist

Including introduction videos and language certificates (B1/B2, Goethe Institute).

Introduction & contract signing

Digital or in person, including registration of the training contract with the Chamber (IHK).

Visa, work permit, travel

We handle the organisation of entry formalities.

Intercultural briefing

For the receiving team, before the apprentice arrives.

Arrival & first weeks

Arrival support and guidance through the settling-in period.

Ongoing check-ins

Months 1, 3, 6 and 12 with both company and apprentice, with a clear escalation path if issues arise.

First contact

Through our partner institute, social media, or a referral/alumnus.

Suitability & motivation interview

Honest expectation-setting on pay, work and life in Germany.

German course up to B1/B2

At our partner institute, alongside vocational and cultural preparation.

Matching & interview

With a suitable training company, held digitally.

Contract, visa, travel

Application at the German embassy, travel organisation.

Arrival in Germany

Support finding housing, dealing with authorities, opening a bank account.

Ongoing support

Buddy programme and regular check-ins throughout your training.
