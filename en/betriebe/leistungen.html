For Training Companies

Services Overview

The Apprenticeship Success Partnership: more than plain placement — active risk management, intercultural onboarding, and a focus on segments other agencies barely serve.

Core services

What's included in the success fee

Needs analysis & matching

We take your requirements profile and pre-screen suitable, language-tested candidates (B1/B2, Goethe Institute).

Intercultural onboarding

Not just the apprentice is prepared — the receiving team gets a short briefing too. Noticeably reduces friction in the first months.

Active dropout-risk management

Regular check-ins in months 1, 3, 6 and 12 with apprentice and company — problems are caught early, not once it's too late.

Replacement guarantee

If a candidate drops out within a defined period (e.g. 6 months) for reasons beyond the company's control, we provide a low-cost or free replacement.

Transparency dashboard

A digital overview of applicant status, visa progress and training start — lightweight to begin with, later its own portal.

A focus on technical trades too

Active outreach to industrial and skilled-trade companies — a segment established agencies barely serve.
