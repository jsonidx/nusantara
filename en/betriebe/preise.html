For Training Companies

Pricing & Success Model

A success fee, not upfront payment — you pay only once the training actually starts.

Core model

Success fee per placement

Due only after training starts, typically in the range of €3,000–6,000 per placement, optionally staggered: partial payment on contract signing, remainder after training start or the probation period.

The initial consultation and needs analysis are free and non-binding.

Included

What's covered by the fee

  • Matching & pre-screening
  • Intercultural onboarding
  • Check-ins in months 1/3/6/12
  • Replacement guarantee under defined conditions
  • Access to the transparency dashboard
